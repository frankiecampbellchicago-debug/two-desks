The brief

United Revival Automotive is an auto repair shop with loyal customers and an outdated website. They wanted a modern site without losing what their customers already know them by: their colors, their style and their name.

What we built

  • The same brand, made modern

    We kept their orange, black and silver and the fonts from their old site, and rebuilt the layout around them.

  • Reviews up front

    Real reviews from their Google listing, shown in a slider, because trust is the first thing people look for in a mechanic.

  • Hours and a number you can tap

    Opening hours are easy to find, and on a phone the number is one tap away.

  • A real photo gallery

    Twenty-one photos from the shop itself, in a gallery that opens full screen.

  • Every service listed

    Each repair service with a clear description, so customers know they're in the right place.

The design

Bold and confident, like the shop itself: a full-width photo, a big headline and a clear button to get in touch, following a layout the client chose.

Built for phones and Google

A fast, lightweight site that works on any phone, with clear headings and descriptions that tell Google what the shop does.
