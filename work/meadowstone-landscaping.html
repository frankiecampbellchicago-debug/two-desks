The brief

A sample site showing how a higher-end landscaping company could look online. People spending real money on their property want to see the quality of the work before they call.

What we built

  • Recent projects

    Large photos of patios, garden beds, stone walls and outdoor lighting, so the work speaks for itself.

  • Services around the property

    Each service explained in plain language, from design to upkeep.

  • Reviews and common questions

    What clients say, and answers to the questions people ask before they hire.

  • A free consultation request

    A simple form to ask for a free consultation, at the end of the page.

The design

Calm, spacious and premium, with plenty of room for the photos.

Built for phones and Google

Clear sections for each service and the area served, which is what people search for when they look for a landscaper. It works just as well on a phone.
