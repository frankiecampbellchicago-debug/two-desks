The brief

Star Project is a clothing label that releases its pieces in drops. They wanted an online store that feels like the brand, not like a template.

What we built

  • An animated intro

    A comet flies across a sky full of stars and writes the brand's name.

  • A flip-book catalogue

    The catalogue opens like a book, one drop per spread, so each collection gets its own moment.

  • Shopping bag and checkout

    Customers pick a size, add to the bag and check out with an order summary and shipping details.

  • A sign-up list

    Fans join the list to hear first when the next drop lands.

  • The brand's story

    A page telling the story behind the label.

The design

Night sky and gold, with the catalogue as the centerpiece, so every part of the shop feels like part of the brand.

Built for phones and Google

Product photos come in two sizes and load fast, so the store stays quick even on a phone connection.
