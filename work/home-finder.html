The brief

A sample site for a real estate agency, built for both sides of the business: buyers looking for a home, and sellers who want to know what theirs is worth.

What we built

  • Home search

    Buyers filter homes by neighborhood, type and price.

  • Step inside

    Featured homes open through an arched doorway as you scroll, so browsing feels like a visit.

  • A page for every home

    Photos, highlights, a monthly payment estimate and similar homes.

  • Tour requests

    Buyers book a tour straight from the listing.

  • Free valuations

    Sellers ask for a free valuation of their home.

The design

Warm and editorial, with big photos and a calm layout that lets the homes stand out.

Built for phones and Google

Every home has its own page, which gives Google more to list and gives buyers a link they can share.
