More than a website

The website is the start. We also set up the tools around it, so customers can find you, book you, pay you, and come back.

  1. Get found

    Show up when people nearby search for what you do.

    Google Business ProfileGoogle reviewsLocal searchInstagram & Facebook links

  2. Get booked

    Let customers book a time, ask for a quote, or reserve a table without picking up the phone.

    Online bookingQuote formsReservationsText reminders

  3. Get paid

    Take payments, deposits, and orders online.

    Online storeDepositsGift cardsInvoices

  4. Bring customers back

    Emails that go out on their own: a welcome for new customers, promotions and sales, reminders, and a request for a review after each job.

    Email promotionsWelcome emailsRemindersReview requests

    A monthly plan. We host your site, make your edits, and fix anything that breaks, so you never have to touch it.

    HostingEdits and updatesMonthly visitor report

We set up tools you may already know, like Square, Shopify, Calendly, and Mailchimp, and connect them to your site.

By industry

What it looks like for your business

A few examples of what we'd set up, depending on what you do.

Salons, barbers, and spas

Online booking with text reminders, deposits to cut down on no-shows, gift cards, and a review request after each visit.

Restaurants and cafés

A menu with a QR code for the table, online ordering or reservations, a catering form, and emails for specials.

Cleaners, landscapers, and trades

A quote form, a page for each area you serve, a review request after each job, and invoices you can pay online.

Shops and boutiques

An online store, sale and new-arrival emails, and a reminder email when someone leaves items in their cart.

Gyms, studios, and trainers

A class schedule, memberships and payment plans, and a welcome email series for new members.

Auto and repair shops

Appointment requests, service reminders like "time for an oil change", and reviews on Google.
